# What Is a Microneedling Pen? A Complete Buyer's Guide

A microneedling pen is a handheld skin treatment device that uses a row of fine needles moving rapidly up and down to create controlled micro-injuries in the upper layer of the skin. It is also commonly called a derma pen. Both terms describe the same category of electric, pen-shaped tool used for collagen induction, scar improvement, and general skin texture work. This guide explains how a [microneedling pen](https://tbphp.shop/) works, what to look for when buying one, and how needle depth and cartridge choice affect results.

## How a Microneedling Pen Works

A microneedling pen works through a process called percutaneous collagen induction. The needles create tiny, controlled punctures that trigger a wound-healing response. The skin responds by producing new collagen and elastin, which can smooth the appearance of scars, fine lines, and uneven texture over a series of treatments.

Unlike a manual derma roller, a microneedling pen uses a motorized, vertical stamping motion. This makes needle depth more consistent because it depends on the device setting rather than hand pressure and angle.

## Key Features to Compare

When comparing microneedling pens, focus on these specifications:

- Needle depth range, typically 0.25 mm to 2.5 mm
- Number of speed levels, commonly six or seven
- Wireless versus corded operation
- Cartridge compatibility and pin count
- Battery life and charging time
- Weight and grip for maneuverability

A well-built device should use a single-use sterile cartridge that is replaced before every session. Reusing a cartridge increases the risk of infection and reduces needle sharpness.

## Needle Depth Explained

Different depths treat different concerns. The table below is a general reference.

| Depth | Common Use |
| --- | --- |
| 0.25 mm | Absorption and very mild texture |
| 0.5 mm | Fine lines, mild pigmentation, product delivery |
| 1.0 mm | Moderate scars and general rejuvenation |
| 1.5 mm | Deeper acne scars and stretch-mark texture |
| 2.0–2.5 mm | Professional, clinic-level treatments |

Deeper settings are generally used only by trained practitioners. At-home users usually stay at 0.25 mm to 0.5 mm and should always follow the manufacturer's instructions.

## Cartridge and Speed Basics

Cartridges come in different pin counts, including 12, 24, 36, 42, and nano configurations. A higher pin count creates more punctures per pass, while nano tips are designed for gentler treatments. Speed refers to how many vertical oscillations the motor completes per minute; higher speeds can feel smoother and produce more consistent coverage.

## What the Evidence Shows

Clinical research supports microneedling for atrophic acne scars and skin rejuvenation. A foundational review by Singh and Yadav describes microneedling as a widening field with applications in scarring and skin texture (PMID 27559496). More recent reviews describe microneedling as a mechanically induced skin-renewal process (PMID 42152593). A 2026 systematic review comparing microneedling with chemical peels found microneedling to be an effective option for atrophic acne scars (PMID 42281674).

These findings describe the device category rather than any single brand. Results vary by device quality, needle depth, operator experience, and number of sessions.

## Safety and Aftercare

Because microneedling creates micro-channels in the skin, hygiene is essential.

- Use a new sterile cartridge for every treatment.
- Clean and disinfect the device before and after use.
- Avoid treatment over active acne, infection, or broken skin.
- Apply sunscreen and avoid direct sun exposure afterward.
- Do not share a device or cartridge with another person.

## FAQ

**Is a microneedling pen the same as a derma pen?**
Yes. Microneedling pen and derma pen are two names for the same electric pen-shaped skin needling device.

**What depth should a beginner use?**
Most guidance suggests starting at 0.25 mm to 0.5 mm and increasing slowly. Deeper settings should be left to trained professionals.

**How often can I use a microneedling pen?**
Frequency depends on needle depth. Shallower treatments can be repeated more often, while deeper treatments need longer recovery between sessions.

**Can the pen be reused?**
The pen body is reusable, but the needle cartridge is single-use and must be replaced before every session.
